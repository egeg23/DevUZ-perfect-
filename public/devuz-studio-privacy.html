DevUz Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the DevUz Studio app receives, including information from Google accounts, and how that information is used, stored, shared and deleted.

1. Who we are

DevUz Studio is a marketing analytics dashboard developed and operated by IP Maksimov Egor Andreevich (DevUz Studio), 138 Shota Rustaveli Street, Tashkent, Republic of Uzbekistan. We are the controller of the data described in this policy. Contact: Telegram @Devuz_studio_bot.

2. Information the app receives

When a Google account is connected to DevUz Studio on Google's consent screen, the app receives the following information from Google:

  • Google Ads data (permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords): keyword ideas and their average monthly search counts from Keyword Planner; and the names, settings and performance statistics (spend, impressions, clicks) of campaigns in the Google Ads accounts the connected Google account can access.
  • Google Analytics data (permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly): the list of Google Analytics properties the connected account can view and aggregated traffic reports of the selected website (visits, pages, traffic sources). This access is read-only.
  • Account information: the email address of the connected Google account, so the dashboard can show which account is connected, and the access tokens that Google issues to the app.

The app does not receive the content of Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, Photos or any other Google service.

3. How we use the information

Information received from Google is used only to provide the features of the dashboard:

  • to show keyword statistics and to choose topics and headings for the pages and articles of our own website;
  • to show the reports of our own Google Ads accounts and to manage our own advertising. A campaign is changed only when a member of our team presses a button in the dashboard;
  • to show the traffic reports of our own website.

We do not use Google data for any other purpose. We do not use it to serve advertising, to build profiles of people or to train AI models. The app does not create, edit or generate images, video or audio.

4. How we store and protect the information

  • Access tokens issued by Google are kept in encrypted storage and are never displayed.
  • Reports are shown only to authorised members of our team.
  • Keyword statistics that were used to plan an article are saved together with that article.
  • All connections between the app, its users and Google use HTTPS.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell information received from Google and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is disclosed only in these cases:

  • Our hosting and database providers store the data on our behalf so that the app can work.
  • Search phrases may be passed to our AI text provider, Anthropic, solely to provide the features of the app: keyword ideas with their monthly search counts, to draft an article for our own website; and the search queries that triggered our own ads, to find irrelevant queries and to draft ad texts. These phrases contain no personal data. Google Analytics reports are not passed to AI providers. Google data is never used to train AI models.
  • When the law requires us to disclose information.

DevUz Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep the information for as long as the Google account stays connected to the app. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or with the disconnect button in the dashboard. After access is revoked, the data received from Google is deleted within 30 days, or sooner on request.

7. Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent to its processing. Write to us in Telegram at @Devuz_studio_bot. We reply within ten working days.

8. Children

DevUz Studio is a business tool. It is not intended for children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.

10. Other policies

The privacy policy of the devuz.studio website, which covers enquiries, the chat and orders on the site, is at devuz.studio/en/privacy.
