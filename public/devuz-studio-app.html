DevUz Studio

DevUz Studio is a marketing analytics dashboard. It brings Google keyword research, Google Ads reports and Google Analytics traffic into one place, so a team can decide what to publish on its website and see how its advertising performs.

What DevUz Studio does

  • Keyword research. For a search phrase such as "website development Tashkent" the app shows related phrases and how many times a month people search for them on Google, by country. These numbers are used to choose topics and headings for website pages and articles.
  • Advertising reports. The app shows the campaigns, spend, impressions and clicks of the connected Google Ads accounts.
  • Website traffic. The app shows visits, pages and traffic sources of the connected website from Google Analytics.

DevUz Studio is built and used by the team of DevUz Studio, a web development studio in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, to plan the content and advertising of its own website, devuz.studio.

Why the app asks for access to a Google account

The numbers above come from Google. To show them, a member of the team connects a Google account on Google's own consent screen. DevUz Studio asks for two permissions:

  • Google Ads (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). Used to read Keyword Planner statistics and the reports of the connected Google Ads accounts. A campaign is changed only when a team member presses a button in the dashboard.
  • Google Analytics, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly). Used to read the traffic reports of the connected website. The app cannot change anything in Google Analytics.

DevUz Studio does not ask for access to Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, Photos or any other Google service.

How Google data is used

  • Google data is used only to show the reports described on this page and to plan the content and advertising of the team's own website.
  • Google data is not sold and is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Google data is not used to build profiles of people and is not used to train AI models.
  • DevUz Studio works only with numbers and text reports. It does not create, edit or generate images, video or audio of any kind.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

DevUz Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
